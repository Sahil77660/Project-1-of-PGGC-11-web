HCP - Heritage and Culture of Punjab

Subject Code: PUNC101

Heritage and Culture of Punjab introduces students to the rich history, culture, traditions, heritage, and literature of Punjab.

Topics Covered

  • Introduction to Punjab
  • History of Punjab
  • Punjabi Culture and Traditions
  • Folk Music and Dance
  • Fairs and Festivals
  • Historical Heritage of Punjab
  • Art and Literature of Punjab
