Fundamentals of Mathematical Statistics

Subject Code: BCA-102

Fundamentals of Mathematical Statistics introduces the basic concepts of statistics and helps students understand, organize, analyze and interpret data.

Topics Covered

  • Introduction to Statistics
  • Collection and Presentation of Data
  • Measures of Central Tendency
  • Measures of Dispersion
  • Probability
  • Correlation
  • Regression
