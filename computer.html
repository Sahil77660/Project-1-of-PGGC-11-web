SEC - Computer Fundamentals & Personal Computing Software

Subject Code: CSAS101

Computer Fundamentals and Personal Computing Software introduces students to the basic concepts of computers, their components, operating systems, and commonly used computer software.

Topics Covered

  • Introduction to Computers
  • Types of Computers
  • Computer Hardware
  • Computer Software
  • Operating Systems
  • Microsoft Word
  • Microsoft Excel
